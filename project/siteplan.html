Smart Budget Hub - Site Plan

WDD 131 Web Fundamentals Project Plan

1. Site Name

Proposed Name: Smart Budget Hub

Reasoning: This name directly reflects the website's purpose—providing accessible financial tools, budget frameworks, and spending trackers to help users make informed money decisions.

Optional Domain Availability: smartbudgethub.org

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Smart Budget Hub is to empower individuals and students to build financial literacy, establish healthy savings habits, and analyze monthly expenses. The site offers structured budgeting guides, interactive JavaScript expense-tracking tools, and personalized budgeting consultation inquiry options.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "How can I split my monthly income between savings, necessities, and personal spending using the 50/30/20 budgeting rule?"
  • Scenario 2: "How do I calculate and save my monthly expenses directly on the site without losing my data when I refresh the browser?"
  • Scenario 3: "Where can I contact a financial advisor to ask specific questions about my personal budgeting goals?"

4. Color Schema

The selected color scheme creates a clean, trustworthy, and modern financial aesthetic:

Deep Navy Blue
#0d3b66
(Header, Footer, Headings)
Emerald Green
#00a86b
(Buttons, Highlights, Accents)
Soft Amber/Gold
#f4d35e
(Special Calls to Action)
Warm Cream
#faf0ca
(Page Background)

Application Rules:

  • Primary (#0d3b66): Main headings (H1/H2), navigation bar background, and structural borders.
  • Secondary (#00a86b): Subheadings (H3), buttons, active links, and positive financial readouts.
  • Accent (#f4d35e): Highlighting key stats, notice banners, and hover states.
  • Background (#faf0ca): Overall page background to reduce eye strain compared to pure white.

5. Typography

  • Headings Font: Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif — Clean, sharp, modern sans-serif fonts for professional readability on all screens.
  • Body Font: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif — Neutral, highly readable font for comfortable long-form reading and data tables.
